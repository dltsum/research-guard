# Code of conduct

Research Guard contributors are expected to collaborate with respect,
specificity, and scientific honesty.

## Expected behavior

- Critique methods and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and provenance limits.
- Respect privacy, embargoes, licenses, research participants, and human-owned
  decisions.
- Welcome good-faith correction and document changed conclusions.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
  misinformation, and publication of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not place sensitive
details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit
participation, or ban repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions
should protect reporters' privacy, document the relevant behavior, and avoid
retaliation.

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, discussions, community
spaces, and public representation of the project.
