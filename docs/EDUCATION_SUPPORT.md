# Education and educational-technology support

Research Guard has separate `education` and `educational_technology` profiles.
The main agent selects one explicitly and records its rationale. Request text is
not passed to a keyword classifier or a small routing model.

## Shared evidence boundary

- A venue list is only a discovery route. Before copying headings, structure,
  length, templates, citation style, or narrative conventions, retrieve current
  official evidence for the exact venue, year, track, and submission stage.
- Every literature lead must retain a clickable HTTPS DOI or original-record
  link. Index membership (SSCI/SCI/CSSCI/C-journal/IEEE) is verified separately
  and never inferred from a journal website.
- A changed method or bound discipline profile invalidates prior collision
  evidence and requires the full novelty route again.
- Learners, classrooms, teachers, schools, institutions, courses, and platform
  deployments are different units. Nested or repeated observations are not
  independent replicates.

## Data and measurement

Public discovery routes include [NCES DataLab](https://nces.ed.gov/datalab/onlinecodebook/),
[IPEDS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipedspas/),
[OECD PISA data](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/programmes/pisa/pisa-data.html),
and [UNESCO UIS data](https://www.uis.unesco.org/en/data).

Before analysis, record the sampling design, weights, strata/PSUs, replicate
weights, plausible values, imputation, exclusions, disclosure controls,
construct definition, reliability, validity, invariance, and item functioning
that apply to the claim. Reliability does not establish validity. Engagement,
clicks, time-on-task, completion, and platform activity are not learning
outcomes unless the measurement argument establishes that relation.

## Metric routing

The core metric engine accepts only independent-run CSV data. Education records
normally require a specialist analysis when they are participant-level,
clustered, longitudinal, weighted, psychometric/IRT, or qualitative. Register
that analysis in the experiment/preregistration contract; do not aggregate away
the design to make the core tool accept it.

Technology evaluations also freeze train/validation/final-test separation at
the level implied by the claim: learner, class, course, teacher, institution,
time period, or deployment. Predictive performance alone cannot support a
causal claim about learning, instructional efficacy, or deployment benefit.
