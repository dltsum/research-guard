# Paper spine and titles

Preserve the observed phenomenon, formulate the broader research problem, and name
the unifying mechanism. Connect predictions to cross-context evidence and possible
falsifiers. Return the requested unranked macro, meso and local title candidates;
the user chooses the final title.

Use `spine_action=plan/register/status` when a stored plan is useful. Register against
the current plan by ID; no plan or method checksum is required. Method revision and
title refinement remain ordinary edits. Existing novelty reports can be attached
with `spine_action=bind_collision`; support the conclusion with actual source links
and recorded scope, not a signed receipt or matching hashes.

The helper checks a usable problem/mechanism/evidence structure. It does not invent
a contribution, infer universal novelty, or shrink the question merely because
related work exists. Missing novelty evidence remains an explicit research task.
